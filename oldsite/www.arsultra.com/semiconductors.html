RAD-HARD Semiconductors

ARSULTRA S.A. and RedCat Devices Inc. with a strategic partnership are developing and providing special rad-hard devices for the space and harsh environment industry.
RedCat Devices (RCD) is a fabless semiconductor company devoted to design semiconductor memories and analog devices for aerospace and nuclear science taking the best from standard and well consolidated CMOS technologies using radiation hardening by design (RHBD) proprietary methodologies to enhance resistance both to total ionizing dose (TID) and single event effects (SEE).
