OpenVpx ITAR FREE LEON3 Fault Tolerant CPU for Space Applications.

Flexible system design based on a RAD-HARD CPU Board under OpenVpx standard.

The enclosure was developed compliant to the Open VPX standard, ANSI / VITA 46,48 & 65 and designed with the 3U form factor conduction cooling heat dissipation.

The modules design (plug-in unit module) for 3U format, is based on the one that corresponds to heat dissipation by conduction cooling, following Open VPX standard. This plug-in unit module, was designed for Rear Side 1" of pitch.

We implement card lock retainers in order to ensured the correct assembly and thermal interface for electronics modules and the main enclosure.
