Business


ARSULTRA S.A. is mainly focused on two very clear segment of business. The core of the company is devoted on research and development of:


1) Rugged Computers for Space Applications and
2) Rugged Special Industrial System for Harsh Environments.


With the heritage and knowledge in the above fields, the company can extend capabilities to special research and development projects, with a variety of advanced modeling, simulations, analysis, design and development, qualification and acceptance testing on both fields.
